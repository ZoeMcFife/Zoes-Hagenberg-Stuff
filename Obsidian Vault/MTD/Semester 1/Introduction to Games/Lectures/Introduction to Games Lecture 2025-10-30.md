# Level Design

Level Design = Design of Game Space

>*”Spaces are boundaries for players to move and interact”*
>*- The Level Design Book*

>*”Level designers, or map designers are the individuals responsible for constructing the game spaces in which the player competes.
>
>As such, the level designer is largely responsible …”*

**Game Designer**

- Designs and formulates rules

**Level Designer**

- Designs the player’s encounter with rules
- interprets rules into play
- turns systems into experience

**Game Designers** → more what and why?
- Grammer

**Level Designers** → how and where?
- the sentences

## What is a level?

> Game Design + Level Design + Environment Art

**Level Design** → blockouts and layout

**Environment Art** → Concept Art, modelling, lighting texturing

**MDA Framework**:
- **Game Design** (Mechanics)
- **Level Design** (Dynamics)
- **Environment** (Aesthetics)

Level Design = Design of Game Spaces + Experiences
